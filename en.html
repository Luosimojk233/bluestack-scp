BlueStack SCP: Secret Laboratory

蓝栈 SCP：秘密实验室

Welcome to BlueStack. Our server preserves the core SCP:SL experience while adding a real-time status bar, a score system, round MVPs, global player messages, and mid-round joining.

Rules Features Score System Victory Rewards Commands Contact

Server Rules

  1. Cheats, scripts, and other unauthorized tools are prohibited.
  2. Malicious exploitation of bugs or unintended mechanics is prohibited.
  3. Malicious friendly fire, team-killing, and deliberate sabotage are prohibited.
  4. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and targeted abuse are prohibited.
  5. Do not spam or misuse the global message system.
  6. Please cooperate with reasonable administrator investigations and actions.
Administrators may issue warnings, mutes, kicks, or bans depending on the severity of a violation.

Server Features

  • Infinite stamina Players can continue sprinting without waiting for stamina recovery.
  • Infinite reserve ammunition Weapons have unlimited reserve ammunition, but magazines must still be reloaded.
  • Mid-round joining and automatic respawn Players joining during a round are automatically assigned a suitable role and respawned.
  • Class-D starting items Class-D personnel receive one coin and one bottle of painkillers when they spawn.
  • Real-time status bar Displays your name, role, latency, score, daily playtime, and the number of living players.
  • Score, assist, and round MVP systems Kills, assists, victory rewards, and round performance are tracked automatically.
  • Global player messages Players can submit queued messages for the entire server.

Earning and Losing Points

New player score 10 points

Automatically initialized when a player first uses the score system.

Regular enemy kill +10

Defeat a regular member of an enemy faction.

Defeat an SCP +15

Complete a valid kill against an SCP role.

SCP defeats a human +5

An SCP completes a valid kill against a non-SCP player.

Valid Class-D kill Additional +1

Class-D personnel receive an additional valid-kill bonus.

Valid assist +3

Damage the target within 60 seconds before death without being the final killer.

Regular death -5

Death caused by a valid enemy attacker.

Suicide or environmental death -5

Death caused by suicide or the environment.

Killed by a teammate No deduction

The victim of a team-kill does not lose points.

Team-killing No reward

Grants no points and does not count as a valid kill.

The minimum score is 0. Scores cannot become negative. Multiple hits by the same player against the same target only count as one assist.

Victory Rewards

Foundation victory +20

Only surviving members of the Foundation faction are rewarded.

Chaos victory +20

Only surviving members of the Chaos faction are rewarded.

SCP victory +30

Only surviving SCP players are rewarded.

Human nuclear victory Additional +5

An additional nuclear victory reward for eligible human winners.

Only living members of the winning faction receive victory points. No reward issued if the winning faction cannot be determined.

Friendly Factions

Foundation faction

Foundation forces, Facility Guards, and Scientists are friendly.

Chaos faction

Chaos Insurgency and Class-D personnel are friendly.

SCP faction

All SCP roles are friendly with one another.

Friendly fire does not count toward assists, and team-killing grants no points.

Round MVPs

Performance Score MVP

Calculated from valid kill and assist points. Victory rewards and administrator adjustments are excluded.

Kill MVP

Calculated from valid kills during the round. Multiple players may share the award if tied.

Player Commands

.bc message

Send a global message. Each message is displayed for five seconds. Each player has an individual ten-second cooldown.

.zs

Kill your current character. The normal death point deduction applies.

.sc

Enable or disable the real-time status bar.

Press ~ to open the in-game client console. Commands must begin with a period, such as .sc, not /sc.
